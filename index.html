About B.Tech (Hons) Software Engineering — HIT

I build software that has to work in the real world — for governments, enterprises, and everyday users who can't afford it to fail.

Over 4+ years at LADS Africa I've helped design and ship platforms now used by more than 60 local authorities — owning features end to end, from database schema and REST APIs to the UI and deployment. I'm comfortable across the stack: Laravel and Django on the backend, React and Next.js on the frontend, Flutter for mobile, and Python and TypeScript for data and services.

I care about clean architecture, reliable systems, and details that hold up under load — whether that's an offline-first sync engine, an IAS 16 accounting module, a time-series fuel-loss detector, or a fraud engine guarding a banking kiosk.
